ABOUT US
Keep the content concise, credible and project-oriented.
Who We Are
Al Kabir Lighting is an Oman-based lighting and building material trading company serving contractors, consultants, industries, government organisations, developers and project customers. We provide dependable products, commercially competitive solutions and professional project support through a diversified portfolio of lighting, electrical and building material products.
Vision
To become one of the most trusted lighting and building material solution providers in Oman and the GCC.
Mission
To provide reliable products, professional service and commercially competitive solutions while building long-term relationships with customers, suppliers and project partners.

WEBSITE STYLE & DESIGN DIRECTION

Displaying product prices
Too many specifications on category pages

PAGE 18
WEBSITE POSITIONING & FINAL RECOMMENDATION
The website should communicate breadth, professionalism and response capability without becoming overcomplicated.
Recommended Positioning Line
AL KABIR LIGHTING
Lighting  |  Electrical  |  Building Materials  |  Project Solutions
One Partner. Multiple Solutions.
Recommended First-Version Size
Approximately 15–20 main pages are sufficient. Start with the core pages and add individual product pages gradually as priority products and brands are finalised.
Best Content Balance
Approximately 70% visual/product showcase and 30% company/technical information. The strongest sections should be Products, Brands, Sectors, Downloads and Request a Quote.
Final Website Objective
A visitor should understand within seconds that Al Kabir Lighting can support complete project requirements across lighting, electrical and building materials, with strong sourcing, technical assistance and fast commercial response.
